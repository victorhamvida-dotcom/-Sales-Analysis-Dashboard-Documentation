## 1. Executive Summary
This dashboard analyzes **sales performance** across multiple dimensions account managers, states, cities, and months  to provide a comprehensive view of business trends and revenue drivers. It highlights total orders, quantity sold, and revenue generated between **2013 and 2017**, helping management identify top performers and regional opportunities.

## 2. Business Problem
Sales teams often struggle to track performance across different regions and account managers in real time. Without a unified dashboard, it’s difficult to pinpoint which areas or individuals are driving revenue growth.
This dashboard solves that challenge by consolidating sales data into a single interactive view, enabling quick insights into **who sells the most, where sales are strongest, and when demand peaks.**

## 3. Dataset Overview
| Metric              | Description                                                   |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Order Data**         | Includes order number, date, quantity, cost, retail price, and profit |
| **Customer Information**| Name, city, state, and customer type                           |
| **Account Manager**     | Responsible for each sale                                     |
| **Product Details**     | Category, container type, and priority                         |
| **Time Variables**      | Year, month, and day for trend analysis                        |


## 4. Dashboard Workflow
1.	**Data Input:** Historical order data from 2013–2017.
2.	**Data Cleaning:** Standardized city/state names, validated numeric fields, and removed duplicates.
3.	**KPI Computation:** Total orders, quantity, revenue, and profit.
4.	**Visualization:** Interactive charts showing sales by state, city, month, and account manager.
5.	**Insights:** Identifies top performers, regional strengths, and monthly trends.

## 7. Insights
-	**Yvette Biti** leads all account managers with over $154 K in revenue.
-	**NSW** contributes more than two thirds of total sales, making it the strongest region.
-	**Sydney** dominates city level performance, accounting for 68% of revenue.
-	**Sales peaks** occur in February and August, suggesting seasonal demand patterns.
-	**Technology and Office Supplies** categories drive the majority of sales volume.

## 8. Recommendations
-	**Focus on NSW and Sydney:** Allocate more resources to high performing regions.
-	**Replicate top manager strategies:** Analyze Yvette Biti’s and Connor Betts’s sales approaches.
-	**Seasonal planning:** Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns for February and August.
-	**Expand in VIC and WA:** Target underperforming states with promotional offers.
